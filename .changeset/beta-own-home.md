OpenKnowledge Beta now keeps its data and credentials separate from the stable app, in `~/.ok-beta`. After updating, Beta starts fresh: settings, logs, themes, agent chat history, and user config begin empty, and you sign in again. Earlier Beta data in `~/.ok` stays there for the stable app.
