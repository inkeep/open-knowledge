Comment highlights are blue instead of yellow. Yellow is what lint and audit problems use, and what the `==highlight==` mark already wears, so a commented passage was about to be indistinguishable from a warning sitting on the same words. Comments now hold a colour of their own — the passage wash and its underline, the marker in the margin rail, the tint on the card being read, and the highlight on a commented property value all moved together, so the mark still reads as one thing wherever it appears.
