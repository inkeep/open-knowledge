A space you type at the end of a line is no longer lost when a collaborator edits the document before you type the next word.

Before, while someone else was typing, ending a sentence with a space and pausing made the space disappear on the next keystroke from them, so your next word was glued to the previous sentence. The space now stays for as long as your caret sits right after it, and it is saved with the next thing you type.
