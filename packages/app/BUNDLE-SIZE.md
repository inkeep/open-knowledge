# Bundle-size limits — rationale and history

The `size-limit` entries in `package.json` are hand-maintained: `size-limit` ships
no auto-update or ratchet, so each limit is measured and then edited by hand. This
file records *why* each limit moved, so that reasoning survives without living on a
single line of `package.json` that every branch has to append to.

The limits themselves stay in `package.json` — they are read by the
`open-knowledge / size` CI job. Only the narrative moved here.

Combined-chunks 3.78->4.4 MB when the interface-language preference started activating catalogs. The ten non-English locales become real on-demand chunks the moment `ConfigProvider` asks for one; until then nothing imported the loader map and rolldown dropped it, so the chunks did not exist to be measured. They add 580,340 B gzipped, 58,034 B each — every catalog is full-size regardless of how much of it is translated, because an untranslated entry compiles to its English source string, so the skeleton catalogs already weigh what finished ones will. Measured 4.31 MB. The main app bundle is untouched at 395.62 kB against its 485 kB limit and CSS at 8.36 kB against 59 kB, which is the point of splitting them: a reader downloads one catalog, not eleven. Importing all eleven eagerly would have put ~638 kB gzipped in front of first paint, more than the entire main-bundle budget on its own, so there is no reclamation lever here — the weight is the feature.

Combined-chunks 4.4->4.5 MB once every catalog held real translations rather than English skeletons. Measured 4.41 MB, 6.02 kB over. The entry above predicted this would cost nothing: an untranslated entry compiles to its English source string, so a skeleton already weighs a full catalog, and translating it swaps bytes rather than adding them. That holds for Latin and CJK and does not hold for the Indic and Arabic scripts. Measured per-locale gzipped chunks now span 60,026 B to 78,840 B, where before every one sat at 58,034 B: Devanagari and Bengali are the two largest at ~78 kB, Arabic and Urdu next at ~71 kB, and the Latin and Han catalogs stay near 60-65 kB. Those scripts are 2-3 UTF-8 bytes per character against English's 1, and gzip recovers less of that on an alphabet as large and combining-mark-heavy as Devanagari than it does on Han. The main app bundle is untouched at 395.23 kB against its 485 kB limit and CSS at 8.36 kB against 59 kB, so this is entirely on-demand catalog weight: a reader still downloads one catalog, and the one they download is now the one they can read. No reclamation lever — same as above, the weight is the feature.
